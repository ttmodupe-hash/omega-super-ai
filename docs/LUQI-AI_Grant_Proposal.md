# Luqi-AI — Seed Grant Proposal

**Prepared for:** African Development Bank (AfDB) / African Union Innovation Fund / Global Educational Foundations

---

## 1. Project Executive Summary

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Project Name** | Luqi-AI |
| **Geographic Focus** | Sub-Saharan Africa (with global export capabilities) |
| **Requested Funding** | **$250,000** — Initial Seed Infrastructure & Engineering Phase |
| **Objective** | A decentralized Progressive Web Application (PWA) that converts standard mobile devices into high-fidelity technical and engineering laboratories. Lightweight 3D/4D visual engines and specialized AI agents deliver advanced training in software development, networking, cybersecurity, and robotics — without expensive physical computer centers. |

---

## 2. Statement of Need & Impact

Across African public high schools, TVET colleges, and universities, students face a massive barrier to technical education: the extreme lack of physical laboratory equipment. Routers, servers, and microcontrollers are financially out of reach for many institutions.

**Luqi-AI bypasses this limitation** by simulating hardware labs directly on standard smartphones using WebAssembly (WASM). The platform also addresses the student-to-teacher gap through an empathetic, voice-enabled AI companion ("Jarvis") customized with iconic African voices — personalized mentoring in the student's own context and language.

---

## 3. Operational and Business Sustainability

Luqi-AI runs on a cross-subsidization model designed for financial independence — not perpetual donor dependency:

- **The Global Premium Engine:** Institutional subscriptions sold in high-resource international markets fund core platform maintenance.
- **The Sovereign African Tier:** 30% of global revenue is automatically allocated to LLM processing tokens for free public primary and high school accounts across Africa.
- **TVET / University Autonomy:** Vocational modules are available via low-cost micro-transactions through regional mobile money networks (M-Pesa, Flutterwave, PayFast).

## 5. Technical Readiness (Appendix Reference)

The platform's core is already built and tested: multi-agent orchestration with a provable 70/30 human-oversight gate (automated test suite: 50 concurrent payment attempts, zero unauthorized completions), sandboxed virtual lab infrastructure, offline-first PWA architecture for low-connectivity schools, and a defined multi-gateway payment router for five regional markets.
